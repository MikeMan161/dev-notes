*Making your accounts work together - automatically*

Automating your money will be the single most profitable system you ever build.

play offense by building a system that acknowledges our normal human behavior- we get bored, distracted, unmotivated- and uses technology to ensure we're still growing our money

create an automatic money flow to manage your money for you. it will take the accounts you've setup (credit cards, checking/savings/investing accounts) and create automatic transfers so your money goes where it needs to go. this automation helps you not worry about actually budgeting every month, it helps you not have to think a lot about finance, lets you review your investments and think about spending habits a few times a year

## Do more before doing less
front load the work now, then get to benefit for years and years

lets you focus on the fun parts of life, no more worrying about whetehr you paid that bill or if you've overdrawn. youll see money as a tool for getting what you want without the manual drudgery of tracking categories and transferring money from one account to another each week.

## the power of defaults
- people are lazy, they will do whatever requires no work-often at their own financial expense
- the key to taking action is making your decisions automatic
- make your contributions to your savings and investing accounts grow passively, with no action required. by setting up an automatic payment plan, you actually make it difficult to stop the contributions to your retirement account
